# Scene models: sources and licences

## The workbench (plan 0016, ADR 0025)

`game/public/scene/bench.glb` and `assets/scene/blend/bench.blend` are made by `tools/blender/bench.py` and baked by `tools/blender/texture.py` from the project's own procedural materials: the project's own work, no outside asset.

The reference photos in `assets/scene/reference/` (Wikimedia Commons: "Generator Repair Workshop" and "Roadside Mechanic Workshop", both CC BY-SA 4.0) are for matching only, git-ignored and never shipped (ADR 0013).
