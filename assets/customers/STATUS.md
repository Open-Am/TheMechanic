## How they are made

`tools/blender/customer.py` (`-- build`, `-- preview <dir>`, `-- render <dir>`, `-- export`), from MPFB 2.0.18 bodies with the CC0 game_engine skeleton; see the script's header and `assets/customers/LICENCE.md`. Build, bake and save of all three takes about 155 s (man_kaftan alone about 4 minutes in round 6, on a busy machine) on the build machine; the renders about 130 s.

## Rounds

1. First body (`man_young`): skin-tight clothes, a shiny swim-cap hair, glossy skin, 1.89 m tall.
2. Clothes draped from the chest and as tubes on limbs: the radii were maxima from the bone line and caught the buttock and shoulder, giving balloon trousers and shoulder pads.
3. Tubes fitted through slice centres with percentile radii; matte low-cut hair; heights set to 1.57 to 1.74 m. `man_young` reads right. The wrapper and kaftan skirts ballooned: their fit took in the hands hanging at hip height.
4. Skirts fitted to hips and legs only; smaller print motifs; deeper blue kaftan, softer sleeves, lower cap. The woman reads right.
5. Kaftan skirt overlapping the body from the waist, trousers hanging below it, a domed cap. Baked, exported, checked, and matched against the reference photos (`match/`, git-ignored, as they contain third-party photos).
6. 2026-10-12, after the owner's review: `man_kaftan` only. The kaftan rebuilt as a lofted robe from collar to shin (rings reaching as far as the body in every direction, smoothed down the length, never coming in by more than 1.5 mm a ring below the chest) with long vertical folds and lofted sleeves; the torso, upper legs and upper arms under it removed; the robe built at its own density and joined after the body is decimated; its shoulders not weighted to the arms, so they do not hump as the arms come down; a kufi; a salt-and-pepper beard placed from the lips and chin. `woman_market` and `man_young` were not rebuilt: their `.blend` and `.glb` files are byte-identical after `npm run assets`. Game views re-rendered for all three at the new spot.
7. 2026-10-12, after the owner looked at round 6 and wrote "His cap is too big and his sleeves is awkward": `man_kaftan` only. The kufi made close-fitting: its rim follows the skull at 5.8 cm above the eyes at the front and 2 cm at the back, its sides follow the head narrowing a little, its crown 6.8 cm above the rim at the front, 8 mm over the top of the head, with a rounded edge and a fainter pattern. The sleeves fitted to the arm's own surface in every direction round it, so the upper sleeve lies close; they open out from the elbow to a 6 cm cuff at the wrist, hang up to 3.5 cm lower on the arm's underside and back, ripple a little at the elbow, and round over into the shoulder under the robe's edge instead of ending in a flat disc. 8. 2026-10-12, after the owner looked at round 7 and wrote "it is worse. the cap is too big for his head, search the web for humans with that kind of cap and fix the cap": `man_kaftan`'s cap only. Measured on photos of Nigerian men in the hula or zanna (listed in `docs/reference/visual-references.md`, "The cap"), against the brow-to-chin length because the crown of the head is hidden: the cap is 0.7 (Balewa, `cap-09`), 0.77 (Buhari, `cap-15`), 0.78 (`cap-05`) and 1.0 (`cap-04`) of the brow-to-chin length tall; 0.92 to 1.0 of the head's width at the temples, never wider (straight sides, at most a slight flare at the top); its rim 0.19 to 0.28 of the brow-to-chin length above the eyebrows (about 2.5 to 3.5 cm), level with or just above the tops of the ears; level or tilted a little back (Balewa's most); the crown flat or slightly domed, some with a small knob. Round 7's cap was wrong in width: built round a smoothed outline of the head's reach in each direction, it stood about 1 cm out from the head on each side at the temples, and its rim sat 3.7 cm above the brows. The cap is now made from the head mesh itself: the scalp's faces above the rim line are copied and each vertex moved onto a cap whose rim is the scalp's own outline at the rim line plus 3 mm, so it lies on the head all the way round and cannot be wider than the head there. Rim 2.4 cm above the brows at the front, 1.2 cm lower at the back; straight sides narrowing 3 %; 9.9 cm tall, 0.7 of his 14.1 cm brow-to-chin length (the elder's end of the range); a rounded edge and a nearly flat crown; 300 triangles. Compared side by side with `cap-15` and `cap-04` (`cap-compare.png`, local only): the cap's height and width against the face now match Buhari's within about 10 %; ours sits a little lower on the forehead. Still differs: the cap is smooth and hard-edged with a printed pattern, not knitted or embroidered cloth with relief; no knob or seam at the crown; a small crease remains at one corner of the crown.
