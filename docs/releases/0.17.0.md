# 0.17.0

Pulling the cord now shows the mechanic's hand pulling the starter. A fixed generator starts, shakes and smokes; the customer smiles and pays while it runs; the mechanic switches it off before they carry it away. A faulty one coughs and dies (plan 0018, ADR 0027).

## Added

- **The model's moving nodes.** `recoil_starter.handle` (the T-grip), `recoil_starter.rope` (along +X from the housing's outlet), `muffler.outlet` (an empty at the spout) and `base_panel.switch` (the red rocker, pivoting about X), each an empty under its part with its shape as a child mesh, listed in `tools/pipeline/child-nodes.json` and checked by `validate.py` and `check.ts`. 24,218 triangles of 60,000; 1.06 MB of 5 MB.
- **The pull.** The generator turns on the bench by the shortest way if the starter faces more than 40 degrees away; the hand grips the handle and pulls it out 40 cm with a slight rise, the rope stretched to it, and lets it run back in (about 1 s).
- **The run.** The engine catches with a kick, shakes gently on its feet (1.5 mm, 25 Hz), puffs and then hazes from the muffler, with the engine sound looping. It runs on its own for 3 s; then the customer smiles and pays while it runs, the hand takes the notes, presses the red switch (the rocker flips), and the engine winds down over 0.8 s before the customer lifts the stopped generator and leaves. About 7.75 s from the catch to the customer gone, then the result. Without a customer figure the run still plays and the mechanic switches it off.
- **The cough.** A failed pull turns over, coughs twice with dark puffs and stops (1.5 s); the round goes on.
- **Skip.** A tap during the start-up skips to its next step: the catch, the smile, the press, stopped, gone.
- `render/startup.ts` (pure timing), `render/exhaust.ts` (the sprites, a texture drawn in code). Audio gains a looped play and `fade()`. App leaves the `cord`, `start` and `fail` sounds to render, which plays them as the animation reaches them.

## Changed

- The timing of a fixed round's ending follows the owner's clarification of 2026-10-12 (ADR 0027 history): the engine runs a little longer before the customer smiles and pays, about 7.75 s from the catch to the customer leaving, where ADR 0027 first said 4 to 5 s.
- The result screen waits for the ending up to 14 s (was 4 s).
- The `cord` sound plays as the handle comes out, not at the press, and the engine's answer when it is let go (was 0.6 s after the press).

## Known gaps

- The hand's grip on the T-handle and the press on the switch reuse the existing grip and pinch shapes.
- The engine sound is the 2.5 s start recording with its middle looped; a seam may be audible.
- With the recoil starter in the tray the pull button is disabled, so the "cough without a pull" path is reached only in tests.
- The owner has not seen it on the phone; the phone preview has not been republished.
