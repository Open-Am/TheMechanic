# 0.17.0

Pulling the cord now shows the mechanic's hand pulling the starter. A fixed generator starts, shakes and smokes; the customer smiles and pays while it runs; the mechanic switches it off before they carry it away. A faulty one coughs and dies (plan 0018, ADR 0027).

## Added

- **The model's moving nodes.** `recoil_starter.handle` (the T-grip), `recoil_starter.rope` (along +X from the housing's outlet), `muffler.outlet` (an empty at the spout) and `base_panel.switch` (the red rocker, pivoting about X), each an empty under its part with its shape as a child mesh, listed in `tools/pipeline/child-nodes.json` and checked by `validate.py` and `check.ts`. 24,218 triangles of 60,000; 1.06 MB of 5 MB.
- **The pull.** The generator turns on the bench by the shortest way if the starter faces more than 40 degrees away; the hand grips the handle and pulls it out 40 cm with a slight rise, the rope stretched to it, and lets it run back in (about 1 s).
- **The run.** The engine catches with a kick, shakes gently on its feet (1.5 mm, 25 Hz), puffs and then hazes from the muffler, with the engine sound looping. It runs on its own for 3 s; then the customer smiles and holds out the notes while it runs, the mechanic's hand reaches across the bench, takes them and brings them off the bottom of the screen into the pocket, then presses the red switch (the rocker flips), and the engine winds down over 0.8 s before the customer lifts the stopped generator and leaves. About 9.15 s from the catch to the customer gone (with 0.16.x's longer, smiling `pay_leave`), then the result. Without a customer figure the run still plays and the mechanic switches it off.
- **The cough.** A failed pull turns over, coughs twice with dark puffs and stops (1.5 s); the round goes on.
- **The arm.** While the hand takes the notes and pockets them, the forearm comes up from the notes' side of the screen; for the switch press it comes in from the side at the panel's height; it glides between, never jumping (`reachArmSide`, `startupArmSide`, `startupArmLift`). The hand stays on the switch 0.4 s after the press.
- **Skip.** A tap during the start-up skips to its next step: the catch, the smile, the press, stopped, gone.
- The engine sound follows the animation (ADR 0028): the catch and `run-start` as it catches, `run-stop` at the switch press (wound down over the 0.8 s), `run-cut` when the start-up is cut short. The stop that ran 2 s after the ending (0.15.2) is replaced; nothing loops under the result.
- `render/startup.ts` (pure timing), `render/exhaust.ts` (the sprites, a texture drawn in code). App leaves the `cord`, `start` and `fail` sounds to render, which plays them as the animation reaches them.

## Changed

- The timing of a fixed round's ending follows the owner's clarification of 2026-10-12 (ADR 0027 history): the engine runs a little longer before the customer smiles and pays, and the owner's feedback the same day that the money exchange must be seen (the notes taken into the mechanic's hand and pocketed): about 9.15 s from the catch to the customer leaving, where ADR 0027 first said 4 to 5 s.
- The result screen waits for the ending up to 14 s (was 4 s).
- The `cord` sound plays as the handle comes out, not at the press, and the engine's answer when it is let go (was 0.6 s after the press).
