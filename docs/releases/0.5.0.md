# 0.5.0

Customers and their patience.

## Added

- Every round has a customer from `game/public/customers.json`: Mama Ngozi (150 s of patience), Alhaji Musa (110 s) and Tunde (80 s), never the same one twice running (plan 0007, ADR 0015).
- Patience drains one second per second; each failed pull costs 20 s. When it runs out the customer takes the generator to another mechanic and the round ends unsolved: no stars and no total, only time, wrong parts and failed pulls (plan 0007, ADRs 0004, 0015).
- A wrong part costs no patience and draws no reaction, so the customer never hints which parts were unnecessary (ADR 0007).
- A patience meter under the timer, green, then amber below half, red below a fifth. The customer speaks at half and at a fifth, when they leave, and when the engine starts (plan 0007).
- The briefing shows who brought the machine and their arrival line; the result screen shows what they said at the end.
- The walk-out is a pure function of the log and the customer: the reducer refuses anything after it with `customer_left`, and a replayed log walks out at the same moment (plan 0007, ADR 0004).
- `dailyCustomerId` picks the day's customer from the day hash, ready for the daily in Phase 4 (ADR 0005).
- `npm run play` shows a walked round; `npm run verify:phone` forces a walk-out with `?patience=5` in development and checks the reaction and the walked result.
- 213 tests, up from 187.

## Changed

- `apply`, `replay`, `scoreState` and `score` take the customer as an optional last argument; without one nothing changes, so earlier logs replay as before.
- `ScoreBreakdown` gains `outcome` (`fixed`, `walked` or null) and `stars` can be 0.

## Fixed

- Nothing.

## Known gaps

- Every customer line is a draft in Nigerian Pidgin, marked `verified: false`, for the owner or the mechanic to rewrite.
- Patience values and the failed-pull cost are first guesses, to be tuned in real play.
- No 3D customer or hand yet; those are the next two plans under ADR 0015.
- The frame rate was measured only in headless Edge with software rendering.
