# 0.11.1

The toolbox moves off to the left, half off screen, and loses its rag. The owner reviewed 0.11.0's toolbox on 2026-10-12 and wrote "Go with your recommendations in 1 and 2"; recommendation 1 was to put it off to the left so only its open tray and the tools show beside the machine (plan 0012, "Revised after review"). ADR 0021 is unchanged.

## What was wrong

- **The toolbox competed with the generator.** It stood 1.85 machine radii in front of the machine, close to the camera, so it looked bigger than the generator and filled the lower third of the screen. At 360 by 640 half of it was under the Pull cord button, and with the tray at its largest the controls covered the tools too.
- **A grey blob in the box.** The rag was a subdivided, displaced cube that read as a low-detail grey lump at game size.

## Changed

- The toolbox stands on the ground to the left of the machine, level with its front: 0.6 radii towards the default camera, 1.3 to its left, turned 1.2 rad (`TOOLBOX_PLACE` and `groundPlace` in `game/src/render/orbit.ts`). In the default view the left screen edge cuts it, so about a fifth of it shows: the open back tray, the spanner and the screwdriver, and the near end. Nothing of it is drawn over the machine or under the pull cord or a tray chip, from an empty tray to all twelve chips, at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640. With the tray full on 360 by 640 the camera stands back and about two thirds of it shows, just above the Pull cord button.
- The hand's trips still go over the machine, never through it. They are shorter in total: from the default view the air filter cover's legs went from 0.67 to 0.41 m and the muffler's from 1.73 to 1.67 m. The recoil starter's grew from 1.58 to 1.70 m (3.10 to 3.13 s), because its bolts are on the far side and the path climbs over the top either way.
- The arm reaches the toolbox from below it. On the legs to the toolbox the forearm's root slides from off screen at the lower right to off screen at the lower left, and back on the legs to the fasteners, eased so it never jumps (`armSideOnTrip`, `toolboxArmSide` in `game/src/render/handpose.ts`). Before, the arm lay as a long tube from the lower right corner across the whole generator to the toolbox. While the hand is at the toolbox the sleeve now reaches 7 to 21 px into the machine's rectangle on screen in sampled trips (limit 32 px in the phone check). While the hand crosses over the machine, and at the fasteners, the arm still passes in front of it, as before.
- The rag is gone (`tools/blender/toolbox.py`). The toolbox is 1,608 of 3,000 triangles (was 1,800) and 192.0 KB of 200 KB (was 198.4 KB).
- `npm run verify:phone` checks the arm at the toolbox at both sizes, and the new place at every tray size, using stand-in chips with the parts' names, and waits for the machine to settle before the trip that follows. Trips report the length of their legs.

No rule changes (ADR 0004, ADR 0007, ADR 0021).

## Added

- 378 tests, up from 369: where the toolbox stands, and that its middle is at the left screen edge, left of and below the machine, on both phone sizes; the arm's root on the lower left or right, the toolbox's side of the screen, and the arm's side over a trip (on its side at the toolbox, gliding over each leg, never jumping between steps).

## Checked

- `npm test` (378), `npm run typecheck`, `npm run docs:check`, `npm run build`, `npm run size` (190.8 KB of 350 KB), `npm run assets` (toolbox 1,608 triangles, 192.0 KB).
- `npm run verify:phone`, with the arm moved, twice. The first run passed with exit 0: 6 of 6 faults, the toolbox checks at both sizes and every tray size, the default trip at both sizes with the sleeve 10 px (390 by 844) and 6 px (360 by 640) into the machine's rectangle at the toolbox, 0 console errors, 108 fps at 4x CPU throttle. The second exited 1 on the known gap only: "hand: 15% of it went inside the machine, loosening muffler", with the push at its 20.0 cm cap (HIDDEN_DEBUG); at the toolbox the sleeve was 3 px and 15 px in. A run before it stopped early with "diagnostic overlay did not show the fault" while another session's Blender job and browser check ran beside it; it left a headless Edge behind, which was stopped before the rerun.
- Before the arm moved, `verify:phone` failed once on "toolbox: 14% of the hand was hidden on the way to or from the toolbox in the default view"; most likely its trip started while the machine was still gliding back down after the stand-in chips were removed, which the check now waits out (not proven; later runs passed it at 2% to 5%).
- `npm run verify:drop` (ports 5199 and 9299): 96 of 96 removals first time, machine never under the controls, frame rate held, 0 console errors. A run beside the other session's jobs had 93 of 96 and "Frame rate: FELL"; the rerun on a quieter machine passed.
