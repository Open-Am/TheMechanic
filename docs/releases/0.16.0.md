# 0.16.0

The customer now brings the generator to the bench at the start, pays and leaves happily when it is fixed, or storms off with it when they run out of patience (plan 0017, ADR 0026).

## Added

- **Bringing the generator.** After Start the round's customer walks in carrying the generator by its handle and sets it on the bench, then steps back (`arrive`, 3.1 s). The timer and patience start when it lands (2.5 s in), so the arrival costs no time and the daily stays fair (ADR 0005). `app` gains `arrive()` and an `arriving` event; `begin()` is now called by `main.ts` when render reports the landing, or at once when there is no figure.
- **Paying and leaving.** A fixed round ends with the customer holding folded naira notes across the bench (shown 0.55 to 1.45 s), then lifting the generator and walking off (`pay_leave`, 3.6 s). The ending has a hold point after the notes change hands (`setPayHold`), so plan 0018 can run the engine and switch it off there before the customer lifts it (ADR 0027). Payment is only an animation: nothing is counted or saved (ADR 0026).
- **Storming off.** A walk-out ends with every part that was off or loose snapping back on at once, silently, the tray emptying, and the customer shaking their head, snatching the generator and walking off fast (`storm_off`, 2.5 s). The action log is unchanged.
- **Carrying.** While carried, the generator hangs from the customer's right-hand grip point by its handle, turned along the line to the left hand's; it eases onto the bench over 0.3 s as it lands, and is turned back to the landed turn over 0.3 s before an ending reaches for it.
- **Skip.** A tap anywhere during the arrival lands the generator at once and starts the clock; a tap during an ending goes to the result.
- The result screen waits for the ending (at most 4 s, so a missing figure never blocks it).
- The same for every fault: the arrival is identical, and the endings depend only on fixed or walked (ADR 0007).
- Tests: 426, up from 416: the timeline (`render/customerpose.ts`: when the generator lands, is lifted and is carried, when the notes show, the easing, the hold before the lift, the endings for every mood) and the app (the clock does not run during the arrival, Start sends `arriving` once, a skipped arrival begins at once, the daily counts from the landing, a round with no customers begins at Start).

## Changed

- The fixed and walked endings replace plan 0015's `pleased` and `leaving` clips, which stay in the files unused.
- The sound files still download after the first Start, now on `arriving` (the tap that unlocks audio).
