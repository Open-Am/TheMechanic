# 0.19.0

Twelve faults instead of six, and fifteen removable parts instead of twelve: a blocked fuel cap vent, a blocked fuel filter, a stuck float, a broken recoil spring, a weak capacitor and a failed ignition coil, with a fuel cap, a fuel filter and an ignition coil to carry them (plan 0020, ADR 0030). All unverified drafts for the mechanic. The three parts are drawn from placeholders until they are modelled.

## Added

- **Three parts** in `machine.json`: `fuel_cap` (on the tank's top, unscrews two turns), `fuel_filter` (on the tap's outlet under the hose, pulls off, requires the hose), `ignition_coil` (behind the recoil starter, two draft bolts, requires and is hidden by the starter). `fuel_tank` now requires the cap, `fuel_tap` the filter. Allocations in `budgets.json`: 400, 400 and 900 triangles.
- **Six faults** in `faults.json` with complaint, symptom, culprit, bad text, fix, difficulty and a clue for every check (ADR 0030's table written out as sentences), `verified: false`.
- **Difficulty** (1 to 3) on every fault, validated; ADR 0030's draft levels.
- `fuel_flow` also needs the fuel cap and filter on.
- Tests: the new removal orders, the hose going back after the tap and filter, all twelve faults solved from their logs, every two faults differing in at least one clue, the daily drawing from all twelve.

## Changed

- **The daily's fault changes from today.** The daily indexes over the fault list (ADR 0005), so every day's fault from the release day on differs from what six faults gave. A player who already played today keeps the attempt used (ADR 0024). ADR 0030 accepts this.
- `blocked_fuel_tap` now needs the fuel filter off on the way to the tap.
- The `runs_no_output` line reads "The engine runs sweetly. The bulb gives no proper light." so it fits a weak capacitor too.
- **The asset check tolerates parts the model lacks.** `tools/pipeline/check.ts` and `tools/blender/validate.py` now warn, naming each part node (and its fasteners) missing from the model, instead of failing; the game draws those parts from their placeholders. A missing base node is still an error.
- `verify:phone` plays all twelve faults in a fixed order through `?faults=`, and checks the toolbox clear of the controls up to twelve tray chips; past twelve (fifteen chips on a 360 by 640 screen take eight rows) it reports instead of failing (open question for the owner).
