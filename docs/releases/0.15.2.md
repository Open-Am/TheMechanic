# 0.15.2

The engine now sounds like it started: it catches, surges, settles and keeps running, then winds down (ADR 0028, on plan 0013's sound). On 2026-10-12, after playing the preview, the owner wrote: "the generator sound when it starts does not sound like a successful start." The owner accepted ADR 0028 and chose these recordings, writing "Go with the 2 instead".

## Added

- `run.ogg`, the engine running: the idle of a Husqvarna 445 chainsaw (ElSchorscho, Freesound 871135, CC0 1.0, page checked 2026-10-12). The file is 1.15–3.62 s of the original, 12,056 bytes. It plays on repeat from 0.110229 to 2.358750 s into the file, a loop of 2.249 s.
  - The loop points are upward zero crossings, placed and checked on the decoded file.
  - The step across the seam (0.00101) is smaller than the loop's own median step from one sample to the next (0.00354).
  - The level 5 ms either side of the seam is -35.9 and -36.6 dB.
  - The file keeps about 0.11 s of sound either side of the loop, so the codec's edges never fall on the seam.
- Looping in the audio code (`platform/local/audio.ts`): `startLoop(moment, options)` and `stopLoop(moment, { fadeOutSeconds })`, on the Web Audio API with no new dependency.
  - One loop at a time. Starting the loop that is already playing does nothing, and so does a second stop.
  - The loop comes in over the last 0.3 s of the one-shot it follows (the crossfade from the catch).
  - It fades out over the time given, or stops at once. Muting stops it at once.
  - Its nodes are disconnected once it has stopped.
- Three engine cues, passed like moments and wired in `main.ts`: `run-start`, `run-stop` (a 0.8 s wind-down) and `run-cut` (at once). Plan 0018 will call `run-stop` at the switch press.
- `sounds.json` rows can loop (`loop`, `loopStart`, `loopEnd`). `checkLoop` holds a loop to ADR 0028: 1 to 3 s, inside its trim, with no fade reaching into it. A test ties the game's loop points (`RUN_LOOP`) to `sounds.json`. `LICENCE.md` gives the loop points.
- `verify:phone` checks:
  - the winning pull plays `start`, then `run` loops and is still running as the result shows;
  - it winds down by itself, after the loop has come in;
  - nothing loops after the result screen.
- Tests: the loop in a fake audio graph, the cues, the winning pull starting the loop, a new round cutting it, and every `checkLoop` rule.

## Changed

- `start.ogg` is now the same saw catching (ElSchorscho, Freesound 871136, CC0 1.0, page checked 2026-10-12): 2.84–6.80 s, 3.96 s, 21,675 bytes. That covers the first firing at 2.86 s (the two pulls before it are left out), the surge, and the settle to idle, fading over its last 0.3 s into the loop. It replaces esperri 118971 (three revs dying away), which the owner said did not sound like a start. Its vibration is unchanged.
- Levels:
  - The catch's loudest 50 ms is -16.3 dB RMS, in line with the other sounds.
  - Just before it fades, it is -28.9 dB RMS, the same as the loop, so the loop takes over at the same loudness.
- When the engine runs, today:
  - The winning pull plays the cord, then the catch 0.6 s later, and the loop comes in at the catch's end.
  - The engine runs while the customer pays and leaves, and on for 2 s under the result. Then it winds down over 0.8 s, about 6 s of engine from the pull (ADR 0027's timing).
  - A tap that skips the ending, a new round, or mute stops it at once.
  - Plan 0018 moves the stop to the switch press.
- Eleven sound files, 50.7 KB (50,699 bytes) of the 250 KB budget.
