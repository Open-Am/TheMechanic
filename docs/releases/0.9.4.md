# 0.9.4

The hand no longer sinks into the machine when a double tap loosens a part. Since 0.9.3, `npm run verify:phone` failed on main with "hand: 66% of it went inside the machine, loosening muffler" (limit 10%; 42% and 43% on later runs, and some runs passed).

## What was wrong

- **The hand took hold of the part's origin.** When a double tap loosens a part, the hand closes on the spot under the finger, but only if the last press showed that part. Otherwise it fell back to the part's origin, which for the muffler is inside the machine. Before 0.9.3 both taps had to show the same part, so the fallback was never reached from a double tap. Since 0.9.3 the second tap still counts while the first tap's part is under the finger by its hit volume, even if a neighbour shows there. In the failing runs the second tap showed the alternator cover, and the hand went to the middle of the muffler.
- **The machine moved between the two taps.** 0.9.3 frames the machine above the tray and glides it up when the tray grows a row. The glide went on between the two taps of a double tap, so the second tap, at the same screen point, landed on a different spot of the machine. In the failing runs the cover was at 87 of 134 px, still gliding, when the second tap came down. This is what made the second tap show a neighbour.
- **The phone check's retry turn grabbed the machine.** When a part is out of view, the check turns the camera with a drag that started at (290, 300) on "empty background". The machine now sits higher, so that point is on the tank: each retry took hold of the machine and tilted it towards a view from the top, until a part (carburetor, air filter, fuel hose) stayed hidden through all eight retries. Main failed this way in one of three runs.

The screen-to-world mapping under the framing shift was checked and is not at fault: rays come from `setFromCamera`, whose projection includes the view offset, and the orbit maths uses `unshiftNdcY`. With the framing switched off, the hand behaves the same for the same tap.

## Fixed

- A double tap takes hold where a tap touched the part: the second tap's spot if it shows the part, else the first tap's (`doubleTapGrasp` in `game/src/render/gesture.ts`). The part's origin is used only when no spot on the part is known.
- The machine holds still while a double tap could still complete (`awaitingSecondTap`), and carries on settling above the controls after.
- The phone check's retry turn starts at (290, 150), above the machine, with the same 80 by 20 px drag as before.

No rule changes: the same for every part, removable or not (ADR 0007, ADR 0020).

## Added

- 246 tests, up from 244: the double tap's grasp when the second tap shows a neighbour, and the machine holding still between the two taps.

- `npm run verify:drop`: 96 of 96 removals first time, machine never under the controls, frame rate held, 0 console errors.
