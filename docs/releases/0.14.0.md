# 0.14.0

The customer stands across the workbench while you work: Mama Ngozi, Alhaji Musa or Tunde, each a 3D person who waits, checks the time, crosses their arms as their patience runs out, reacts to a failed pull, nods when the engine starts, and walks off if they run out of patience (plan 0015, ADRs 0015, 0023 and 0025).

## Added

- **Three customers**, one file each in `game/public/customers/<look>.glb`, made by `tools/blender/customer.py` from MakeHuman bodies with our own clothes, textures and clips (ADRs 0015 and 0020, `assets/customers/LICENCE.md`). All three are approved by the owner (`assets/customers/STATUS.md`): Mama Ngozi in a wax-print blouse, wrapper, head tie and red slippers; Alhaji Musa in a long kaftan with a kufi and leather mules; Tunde in a T-shirt, jeans and slides. 14,142 to 14,227 triangles of 15,000, 53 joints of 64, 710 to 911 KB of 1.5 MB.
- **Where they stand.** On the floor 0.35 m behind the bench's far edge, facing the mechanic, fixed in the scene (ADR 0025); the generator turns between them and the camera, always nearer (`game/src/render/customerpose.ts`). Seen head to waist: at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640 the head stays below the timer and the face above the generator.
- **Clips** (plan 0015 reading 5) play with one AnimationMixer (`game/src/render/customer.ts`): `idle` from the start, `waiting` at half patience, `annoyed` then `annoyed_idle` near the end, `failed_pull` on a failed pull, `pleased` when the engine starts, `leaving` on a walk-out, then hidden. Only the clock and the cord move them: nothing done to a part does, for any part or fault (ADR 0007). The endings are kept in one place (`ENDINGS`) for plan 0017 to replace.
- **Never picked.** The customer is drawn on its own layer outside the machine, so the pointer, the hover and the hand's collision never see them: a tap on them does nothing, a drag across them turns the generator.
- **Downloading one round ahead** (ADR 0023). `app` gains `customerLook` and `nextCustomerLook`: the next free-play customer is chosen when a round starts and their file downloads during the round; the round's own file is asked for as soon as the round is chosen, behind the briefing. A file that is missing, fails or is late never holds anything up: the round plays without a figure, and a late figure appears in the pose for the current mood. Development only: `?nocustomer=<look>` and `?slowcustomer=<look>:<ms>`.
- **Plan 0017's clips are already in the files** (`arrive`, `pay_leave`, `storm_off`, the hand grips and the notes, ADR 0026); this release does not play them.
- Tests: 416, up from 401 after merging plan 0016: `customerLook` and `nextCustomerLook` (follows the pick, the next is never the current, the next free round plays them, a daily plays the day's customer, null without customers); the spot behind the bench, behind the generator at every turn, clear of the toolbox, the head and face framed at both sizes; `clipFor` for every event and that part events move no one for every part, fault and mood; the customer file check (`checkCustomer`) with the new clips and nodes.

## Changed

- `tools/pipeline/optimise.mjs` takes the texture size as its third file argument, beside `--keep-empty`; the customers are optimised with empty nodes kept (their hand grips).
