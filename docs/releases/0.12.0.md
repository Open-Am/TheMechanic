# 0.12.0

Sound and vibration. Ten CC0 stand-in sounds ship, 22.9 KB in all. They are the first choices from the shortlist, picked by the owner on 2026-10-12, and stay stand-ins until the owner records the real machine (plan 0013, ADR 0018).

## Added

- The ten sounds, in `game/public/audio/`, each a mono Ogg Opus stand-in from Freesound under CC0 1.0, listed with its page, author and trim in `LICENCE.md`. Each licence was checked again on its page on 2026-10-12. The engine start is 0.98 s, not 2.5 s (see Known gaps). Sizes are in bytes.

  | Moment | Sound | Author | Length | Bytes |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `turn` | a single ratchet click | mickyman5000 | 0.11 s | 795 |
  | `give-unbolt` | one clack of a torque wrench | Flares.fr | 0.12 s | 1,095 |
  | `give-unclip` | a plastic buckle clip | slappy13 | 0.17 s | 1,081 |
  | `give-pull` | a rubber bottle cap popping off | CrudeWax | 0.12 s | 828 |
  | `give-scrape` | a short rusty metal scrape | qubodup | 0.16 s | 1,083 |
  | `land` | a metal clonk | Jetrod | 0.40 s | 2,255 |
  | `seat` | a mechanical latch | xkeril | 0.23 s | 1,450 |
  | `cord` | a lawnmower or snowmobile recoil cord | kyles | 0.60 s | 3,558 |
  | `fail` | after a false start of the same kind of engine | kyles | 0.90 s | 4,823 |
  | `start` | a two-stroke chainsaw catching | damsur | 0.98 s | 5,963 |

  Levels are evened out on the encoded files: every file peaks between -1.5 and -8.5 dBFS, and its loudest 50 ms is between -17.4 and -24.9 dB RMS (`assets/audio/STATUS.md`). The owner's other downloads stay in `assets/audio/source/` on the build machine, not committed, for when players may choose between sounds. That needs an ADR first (`docs/open-questions.md`).
- Sound and vibration hooks for ADR 0018's ten moments: `turn`, `give-unbolt`, `give-unclip`, `give-pull`, `give-scrape`, `land`, `seat`, `cord`, `fail` and `start` (plan 0013, ADRs 0018, 0010). Fasteners and unscrewed parts click once a whole turn, coming out and going in. A part gives with the sound of its kind as its freeing step starts. A part dropped on the tray lands. A part put back seats as it reaches home, before its fasteners go in. The cord plays at once, and the failed start or the engine 0.6 s later. A part that cannot come off stays silent and still (ADR 0007).
- Vibration on phones that support it: about 15 ms as a part gives and as it seats, 40 ms for a failed start, and a 280 ms pattern for the engine start (ADR 0018).
- `Audio` on the Web Audio API and `navigator.vibrate`, with no new dependency (ADR 0002). One `AudioContext`, made and resumed by the first tap ("Start"). Every failure is swallowed, so a missing, late or undecodable file plays nothing (ADR 0023).
- The ten files download once, after the first Start, from `game/public/audio/<moment>.ogg`. Nothing is fetched before Start, and nothing waits on them (ADR 0023). In development a missing file gives one console warning that names it.
- A mute button at the top right of the working screen: a speaker, slashed when muted, 44 px, labelled "Sound on" or "Sound off". Muted means no sound and no vibration. The choice is remembered under `openam.muted`, with a memory fallback when storage is blocked (`platform/local/storage.ts`, which the score store now shares).
- `npm run audio`: Blender 4.5 encodes each moment's chosen original, trimmed, faded and set in `assets/audio/sounds.json`, to mono 48 kHz Ogg Opus at 40 kbps. The check then keeps only the Opus stream, copies the files into the game with a `LICENCE.md` written from `sounds.json`, and checks them. Blender's sound mixdown has no Opus codec in 4.5, so `tools/blender/audio.py` renders through its FFmpeg output instead. That output also carries a small Theora video stream, which the check drops. Proved on 2026-10-11 with a synthetic click: one channel, 0.12 s, and Edge decodes it.
- `checkAudio` and a 250 KB `audioBytes` budget in `tools/pipeline/budgets.json` (ADR 0018 adds it to ADR 0014). It checks that each file is mono Ogg Opus with no other stream, that there is one file per moment and no extra file, that the total is within budget, and that each file is listed in `LICENCE.md` as `CC0 1.0` or `own recording`. `npm run audio` fails on any missing file. `npm test` only warns while none ship; once any file ships, it requires all ten.
- `npm run verify:phone` now also checks sound, through a development hook that logs each moment asked for and whether it could be heard, and a recorder on `navigator.vibrate`:
  - nothing under `/audio/` is fetched before Start, and the ten files are asked for once after it;
  - Start unlocks audio;
  - a double tap on the blocked tank fires no moment and no vibration;
  - the alternator cover clicks 8 turns, cracks loose once (15 ms) and lands, and goes back with a seat (15 ms) and 8 turns;
  - the plug cap pops, and the spark plug clicks with no give sound;
  - a failed pull plays the cord then fail (40 ms), and the winning pull the cord then start (under 300 ms);
  - mute holds over a reload, and while muted nothing is audible and nothing vibrates;
  - the walk-out is silent.
- 369 tests, up from 350 on `main` (with plan 0012 merged in). They cover: the fake-context audio (nothing before unlock, while muted, or for a missing or undecodable file; delays; vibration skipped when muted or unsupported); the mute flag and its storage fallback; ADR 0018's table of vibrations; the give for every motion type; silence on a refusal, a blocked part and the walk-out; clicks per turn across frames and in reverse; the mute button's label; and every `checkAudio` rule (stereo, not Opus, not Ogg, a second stream, over budget, a missing licence row, a non-CC0 licence, an extra file, a missing moment), plus the Opus-only stripping.

## Changed

- The browser checks start headless Edge with `--mute-audio`, so they no longer play the sounds through the build machine's speakers. The sound checks read the game's development hook, where a moment counts as audible when it is scheduled on a running audio context with a decoded file, so muting the output changes nothing they see.
- An accepted remove plays `land` (was `remove`). The walk-out no longer plays `fail`, since ADR 0018 lists no walk-out moment (plan 0013 reading 4).
- `safeStorage` and `StorageLike` moved from `platform/local/scores.ts` to `platform/local/storage.ts`, re-exported from `scores.ts`. No change in behaviour.

## Known gaps

- The sounds were trimmed and levelled from their waveforms, not by ear. Nobody has listened to them yet. Things to listen for are in `assets/audio/STATUS.md`: the clonk is clipped in its original; the torque-wrench clack is raised 18 dB; the ratchet click loses some level in the mono mix; and the failed start was picked as the broken-up stretch after the last pull, so it may not be a cough.
- The engine start is 0.98 s, not the plan's 2.5 s. Its original holds two short pull-and-catch attempts split by 0.38 s of digital silence, with no long run, so the second attempt is used (`docs/open-questions.md`).
