# 0.11.0

A mechanic's toolbox beside the generator. Loosening a bolted part, the hand fetches the spanner or screwdriver from the toolbox, undoes the fasteners and puts the tool back (plan 0012, ADR 0021).

## Added

- A red steel cantilever toolbox, open, on the ground below and to the left of the machine in the default view: body, two trays swung out on their arms, lids hanging open, a carrying handle, worn paint with chips, rust and grease, and a rag, a wire brush and sockets in it. The spanner and the screwdriver lie side by side in its back tray, which faces the machine. It is scenery: it is never picked, never blocks the pointer, and is the same in every round (plan 0012, ADRs 0013, 0021).
- Made by `tools/blender/toolbox.py` (`-- build`, `-- preview`, `-- export`), baked by `texture.py` with a new greasy-paint profile, and checked by `npm run assets`: one `toolbox` node with `slot_spanner` and `slot_screwdriver` under it, 1,800 of 3,000 triangles, 198.4 KB of 200 KB, one 1024 px texture set. It is `game/public/hand/toolbox.glb`, loaded with the hand and the tools, and the loading bar counts it (plan 0012, ADR 0021).
- The trip. Double tap (or G) a part held by fasteners: the hand goes from the pointer to the toolbox, takes the right tool, carries it to the first fastener, undoes each fastener, changes tool at the toolbox if a fastener needs the other one, lets the part come free, lays the tool back in its slot and comes back to where you touched the part. Each leg takes 0.35 s at the default view and longer with distance, at most 0.6 s; four bolts take about 3.1 s from the default view. Putting a part back runs the same trip to do its fasteners up. The hand's path goes over the machine, never through it: it stands off the surface until it is clear of the machine's bounds, climbs 5 cm above the top if the straight line would cross the machine, and comes down (plan 0012, ADR 0021).
- While the trip plays, the view still turns and zooms and the clock runs. A new double tap, a drag on a loose part (it turns the view instead) and a put-back from the tray are ignored until it ends (plan 0012, ADR 0021). The trip is the same for every part apart from its own fasteners, so it shows nothing about the fault (ADR 0007).
- 333 tests, up from 311: the toolbox budget and its check; the trip's order (one trip for four bolts, one tool change for mixed bolts and screws, no trip without fasteners, the same trip for the same fasteners, doing up with no freeing); leg times within 0.35 to 0.6 s; the path over the machine from every side, from under an overhang and from a point whose outward direction points in; loading the toolbox and its slots; the loading bar's file count; choosing the spanner's turn on a bolt.

## Changed

- The tools rest in the toolbox, in view, instead of being hidden off screen.
- The part counts as loose once it is free, while the hand still takes the tool back; it can be carried when the trip ends.
- Holding a tool on a fastener, the hand is kept out of the machine as closely as when it holds a part (checked every frame, with more of its points).
- The spanner's handle no longer always lies towards the lower right of the screen. For each bolt it takes the turn about the bolt, of twelve, at which the hand holding it is least behind the machine over its whole stroke. With the old handle, a bolt under the tank's overhang put the hand 27 to 40 cm behind the machine, past `HAND.maxPush` (0.2 m): measured on main at the same view, the muffler's hand was 49 to 100% hidden while it worked. On main the phone check did not see this because it sampled 350 ms after the double tap, while the tool was still on its way.
- When the cord is pulled during a trip, the tool goes straight back to its slot and the fasteners go in quickly, as before.
- Without `toolbox.glb` the tools come from just off screen as in 0.10.0; without `tools.glb` the fingers turn the fasteners, with no trip.
- `npm run verify:phone` checks the toolbox at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640 (in the default view with the tools in it above the controls, drawn clear of the machine), watches the trip in the default view and on the alternator cover (the hand at the toolbox, the spanner on the bolts, back in its slot after, the path never inside the machine's bounds between its stand-offs), and tries a second loosen during the trip. It takes the hand-inside-the-machine sample at the first fastener, where the hand works, as 0.10.0 did at 350 ms. The start screen may take up to 60 s on its throttled first load (it took 22 to 34 s on the busy build machine). `npm run verify:drop` waits up to 5 s for a part to come loose, for the trip.

## Checked

- `npm test` (333), `npm run typecheck`, `npm run docs:check`, `npm run build`, `npm run size` (188.6 KB of 350 KB) pass. `npm run assets` passes: machine 23,658 triangles, 1,038.6 KB; hand 3,980 triangles, 110.9 KB; tools 1,160 triangles, 48.6 KB; toolbox 1,800 of 3,000 triangles, 198.4 KB of 200 KB.
- `npm run verify:drop`: 96 of 96 removals first time, the machine never under the controls, frame rate held, 0 console errors (exit 0). The run before it, on the busy build machine, had one retry (a double tap's two lifts 468 ms apart with 187 ms of input lag) and a frame-rate drop (exit 1).
- `npm run verify:phone` with HIDDEN_DEBUG, two runs one after the other, both exit 0: 6 of 6 faults, forced walk-out, loading, daily and storage checks as before; the toolbox in the default view with the tools above the controls and none of it drawn over the machine at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640; the hand at the toolbox, the spanner on the bolts and back in its slot after, on the recoil starter from the default view and on the alternator cover; a second loosen during a trip ignored; every part with fasteners undone and done up again with the tool; the trip's path inside the machine's bounds on 0 of 26 trips; the hand at most 0% and 2% hidden on the default-view trip's legs; hand inside the machine at most 5% (hovering on the base); 65 and 81 fps at 4x CPU throttle; 0 console errors.

## Known gaps

- On a 360 by 640 screen the toolbox is cut off by the pull cord button: its trays and the tools show between the machine and the controls, its lower half is under the button. With the tray at its largest the controls cover the tools too. The plan asked for the toolbox fully in view with the tray at its largest; on that screen there is no room for it under the machine and the plan's fallback (cropped, lid and tools showing) applies to the empty tray only.
- The toolbox's bounding box overlaps the machine's by 35 to 40 px at its top-left corner, where the machine draws nothing; the check is that no point of the toolbox is drawn over the machine, not that the rectangles are apart.
- The toolbox is 360 mm long, smaller than the 450 mm the plan first read, so that it fits. It is still large on screen, being nearer the camera than the machine.
- A bolt the camera cannot see at all still hides the hand working it: the muffler's from the default view asks for 34 cm, past `HAND.maxPush`, whichever way the spanner lies. The phone check samples the hand when it starts on the first fastener (0.10.0 sampled at 350 ms, before the tool arrived); main sampled at that moment fails the same way (66%, loosening muffler).
- From some turned views the toolbox is behind the machine, and the hand is hidden behind the machine while it is at the toolbox. The hand-inside-the-machine limit is checked where the hand works on the part and on the default-view trip, not at the toolbox from every view.
- Four bolts take about 3.1 s from the default view, a little over the 1.5 to 3 s ADR 0021 expects; turned views take longer (legs up to 0.6 s).
- Two trays where the reference photo has four, plainer arms, a brighter red than most worn boxes and lighter wear than a roadside box in Lagos; the rag is a lump with no folds. Match images (git-ignored) are in `assets/toolbox/match/`.
- The phone preview is not republished by this branch.
