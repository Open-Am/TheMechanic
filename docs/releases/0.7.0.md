# 0.7.0

The mechanic's hand.

## Added

- On every accepted removal, the mechanic's hand reaches in, closes its grip on the part, rides along with the part's motion, and lets go as the part drifts to the tray. On a put-back it brings the part in and pushes it home. It comes in from the side the part comes off, so it reaches around the machine, never through it. A blocked pull shows no hand (plan 0008, ADRs 0007, 0015).
- The hand: a real-size right hand and forearm of a Lagos mechanic, dark brown skin with lighter palms and engine grease on the fingertips, in a faded blue work-shirt sleeve. Made in Blender by script, no outside tool: a joint skeleton through Skin and Subdivision modifiers, with a `grip` morph target. 3,992 triangles, 157.7 KB compressed, within the ADR 0015 budget of 4,000 and 400 KB (plan 0008).
- `npm run assets` exports, optimises and checks the hand (one node named `mechanic_hand`, the grip morph, triangles, size, texture size) and copies it into the game.
- `tools/blender/hand.py`, `assets/hand/STATUS.md`; `texture.py` can now bake any group and has skin, nail and cloth wear; `match.py` gains `key=<shape key>:<value>`.
- `npm run verify:phone` catches the hand gripping mid-pull and checks that a blocked pull shows no hand.
- 226 tests, up from 214.

## Changed

- A removal takes about 0.3 s longer: 0.18 s to reach and grip before the part moves, and the release while it drifts. Input is never blocked.

## Fixed

- The first hand reached from the bottom-right corner of the screen, which for a part at the back sent the forearm through the machine. It now comes from the side the part comes off.
- The machine's light laterite dust washed dark skin out to tan in the bake; skin now gets grease, not dust.

## Known gaps

- The hand is smoother than a real hand (no wrinkles, tendons or veins in the shape), and the sleeve is a tube with ring folds. If it is not believable enough, the next step is the MakeHuman add-on, which needs the owner's approval (ADR 0015).
- One grip shape for every part: on small parts the fingers close around air.
- Only one public-domain photo of working hands was used as reference; photos of a Lagos mechanic's hands would be better.
- The frame rate was measured only in headless Edge with software rendering: 113 fps at 4x CPU throttle with the hand.
