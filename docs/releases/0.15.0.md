# 0.15.0

The generator now stands on a roadside workbench, seen from a standing mechanic's eye level, with room for the customer across the bench. Dragging turns the generator on the bench (plan 0016, ADR 0025).

## Changed

- **The workbench.** A worn wooden bench, its top 0.78 m high and 1.15 by 0.65 m: five planks with gaps on two battens, four legs, low stretchers, nail heads, weathered grey-brown wood with oil stains. It is scenery: never picked, never in the pointer's way. Made by `tools/blender/bench.py` and baked by `texture.py`'s new `wood` profile, 902 triangles and 137.9 KB of its 3,000 and 300 KB, one 1024 px set (`game/public/scene/bench.glb`). Without the file the game draws a plain box-shaped bench (ADR 0025).
- **The camera.** It stands at a standing mechanic's eye, 1.6 m above the floor, on the near side of the bench, and is framed for each screen by `frameBench` (`game/src/render/stage.ts`). At 390 by 844 it stands 2.04 m back and looks down 12.6 degrees; the generator is 60 percent of the width with its lowest point 60 px above the controls. At 360 by 640 it stands back to 2.39 m, so a 1.76 m customer's head line clears the timer, and the generator is 42 percent wide (never under 40). As the tray fills, the camera tilts and stands back so the generator stays above the controls.
- **Turning.** A sideways drag turns the generator on the bench about its upright axis, all the way round if you like; the camera never moves sideways, so the customer (plan 0015) stays across the bench. An up-and-down drag raises the camera, from the default view up to looking 35 degrees down, to see the top. The hand still holds the spot it grabbed and the generator turns to keep it under the pointer (ADR 0020). Pinch and the wheel zoom from 0.7 to 1.4 times the framed distance.
- **The shadow** is baked on the bench top and turns with the generator: a wide soft shadow plus a darker one where the feet stand, 13.3 KB of 64 KB (ADR 0014 as changed by ADR 0025). The floor beyond the bench fades to the background with the bench's own soft shadow.
- **The toolbox** stands on the bench's left end, its back tray towards the camera, cut by the left screen edge, clear of the circle the generator sweeps as it turns (ADR 0021 as changed by ADR 0025). From eye level the tray's wall hid tools lying on its floor, so the spanner and screwdriver now lie across the tray on its rims. The generator sits a little right of the screen's middle so the tools show. The arm still reaches the toolbox from below on the left.
- The diagnostic overlay names the bench (`glb` or `box`). Development hooks `stage()`, `setStage()`, `resetStage()` and `benchRect()` replace `orbit()` and `setOrbit()`.

## Removed

- The orbit camera's functions the stage replaces (`clampOrbit`, `rotate`, `zoom`, `frameAbove`, `unshiftNdcY`, `projectPoint`, `turnToward`, `DEFAULT_ORBIT`, `TOOLBOX_PLACE`, `groundPlace`) and their tests. The same behaviours are tested on the new model in `game/tests/stage.test.ts`.

## Added

- 394 tests, up from 383 at the start of the plan (4 of the new ones came with the sound stand-ins merged from main): the bench's budget and check; the shadow plane matching the bake; the framing at 390 by 844 (55 to 65 percent), at 360 by 640 (never under 40 percent, the head line under the timer) and with a full tray; a sideways drag turning the machine and not the camera; the camera's rise within its limits; the held spot under the pointer as far as the turn allows; a full turn coming back to the start; the bench and the toolbox clear of the turning generator; the toolbox's tools on screen at both sizes; loading the bench and the fallback box.
- `npm run verify:phone` checks the bench under the machine, the generator's share of the width and the head line at both sizes, and that a sideways drag turns the machine and leaves the camera exactly where it was. It starts each part from the default view, as a player turns the machine back to face a part.

## Known gaps

- The hand gap of 0.9.4 is still there at grazing views. With the check's retry turns piling up from part to part, one run reached yaw -1.85 with the camera raised, and the air filter cover, on the front, was seen edge-on. The hand loosening it was 55% hidden behind the tank: it asked to come 25.5 cm towards the camera and is capped at `HAND.maxPush` (20 cm). From the default view and the views a player turns to, it is at most 9%. `HAND.maxPush` and the 10% limit are unchanged.
- At 360 by 640 with all twelve chips in the tray, the controls cover more than half the screen; the generator keeps 40 percent of the height above them and its top goes under the timer.
- The bench is lighter and cleaner than the oil-blackened benches in the Lagos photos, so the generator's black base still reads against it (`assets/scene/STATUS.md`).
- The key light stays put as the generator turns, so it is lit differently from each side, as on a real bench. The back is darker than the front.
- The owner has not yet looked on the phone. The phone preview has not been republished for this release.
