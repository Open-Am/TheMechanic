# 0.15.1

Dropping a part in the tray is easy again. From the owner, playing the phone preview on a real phone on 2026-10-12: "dropping is still very difficult. Can we make the drop easier?" They named two things: "It goes back to machine" (they drag a loosened part down to the tray, let go, and it flies back onto the generator) and "Tray hard to reach" (the tray area is too small, too low, or under the thumb or the Pull cord button). Later the same day: "It's better to make the drop happen when the part is close to the tray rather than insisting it must be over it. Also it should be possible to drop the parts over each other. It readjust after the drop."

## What was wrong

- **The zone was tied to the controls, not to the generator.** A carried part counted as dropped only from 64 px above the pull cord down (since 0.9.3). Since the workbench (plan 0016, ADR 0025) the generator stands low: at 390 by 844 its lowest point is about 650 px and the controls start about 710, so the zone began at the generator's feet and was a thin strip there. Whatever the generator's place, the line did not move with it.
- **Only the finger's last reported point was judged.** A thumb dragging a part down to the tray lifts while still moving, and the screen reports the last spot it touched, short of where the player meant. A release that registered a little above that line, or over the generator's lower edge, sent the part back. This is "it goes back to machine".
- **The visible target was smaller than the real one.** "Drop it here" was drawn only over the tray chips at the very bottom of the screen, under the pull cord, the hardest place for a thumb to reach. Players aimed there and flicked to reach it.
- A cancelled pointer (the browser taking the touch) was judged at the cancel event's own position, which need not be where the finger was.

## Changed

- While a part is carried, the drop zone is the whole lower part of the screen, full width, from 8 px below the generator's lowest point as drawn down to the bottom edge: over the bench's front edge, the pull cord, the hint and the chips. The line is recomputed on every move from the generator's outline on screen, so it follows the view and the tray. It never starts below the controls' top. `dropTarget` in `game/src/ui/format.ts`, a pure function: (finger, generator's screen bounds, screen size, controls, carried part) to "tray" or "back".
- Near enough counts (a magnet): within 9 percent of the screen's height above the zone, 56 to 80 px (76 at 390 by 844, 58 at 360 by 640), a release drops, unless it is over the generator itself. The carried part's middle on screen is judged as well as the finger. The part glides into the tray as before.
- A fast flick down counts where it was heading: moving down at least 0.8 px per ms over the last 80 ms, the release is judged 120 ms ahead (`flickAhead` in `game/src/render/gesture.ts`).
- "Drop it here" is laid over the whole zone, with a dashed top edge; it brightens as soon as the part is near enough to drop. The controls fade behind it so the carried part shows over them, and the pull cord is disabled while carrying.
- Released over chips already in the tray, a part always drops: the carrying finger belongs to the 3D view, so a chip never takes it as a tap or a put-back.
- After a drop the chips glide to their new places in 180 ms and the new chip grows in; nothing overlaps. The machine re-frames above the controls as before.
- A cancelled pointer is judged where it last was.

No rule changes: a tuning of the tray's drop area within ADR 0020. Dropping on the tray removes the part; dropping anywhere else, including over the generator, sends it back, loose. The same for every part (ADR 0007).

## Added

- `npm run verify:drop` now plays every round at 390 by 844 and 360 by 640, with mouse and touch events, dropping where a real thumb does: just below the generator, on the bench's front edge, half-way down the controls, on the "Drop it here" label, a fast flick released above the zone, beside the generator within the magnet's reach, and right on top of the chips already in the tray (1 to 11 of them). Removals 1 and 7 of every round first release the part over the generator's middle, and it must go back, still loose. After every drop the tray must show every chip, on screen, none overlapping or hidden. It keeps the machine-under-controls and frame-rate checks, and saves a screenshot while carrying.
- 432 tests, up from 403 on main... see below.
