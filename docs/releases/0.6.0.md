# 0.6.0

Phase 3b: every part modelled towards the real machine, with baked materials and Lagos wear.

## Added

- `tools/blender/model.py` models all 16 objects from the references: the recoil cover's pressed shell with 28 cut vent slots and the reel behind, the tank's pressed grooves, knurled cap and screwed handle, the louvred muffler shield and flanged inlet, the alternator end's recessed cross, bolts and earth wire, the full 80 mm spark plug, the right-angle plug boot and HT lead, the carburetor's oval flange, linkage arm and spring screws, the fuel tap's oval body and rotary lever, the big engine shroud, and the detailed door and panel. 22,306 triangles, every object inside its allocation (plan 0006, ADR 0013).
- `tools/blender/texture.py` bakes procedural materials and wear into a 2048 px exterior atlas and a 1024 px small-parts atlas: chipped paint with primer and rust, laterite dust, scuffed and faded plastic, a rusty and sooty exhaust, fine pressed and cast grain (plan 0006, ADR 0014).
- `tools/blender/lookdev.py` applies the game's lighting to the Blender scene; `tools/blender/shapes.py` holds shared modelling helpers; `match.py` gains `only=<id>` to compare a single part with its product photo.
- Every part has at least one comparison image; `assets/gen950/STATUS.md` records each part as `textured` with what still differs. Comparison images stay local, because they contain third-party photos (ADR 0013).

## Changed

- The model is 1.01 MB compressed, up from 80 KB for the block-out, within the 5 MB budget and the 3 MB aim (ADR 0014).
- The atlas budget check counts one material's texture set as one atlas, as the modelling brief defines an atlas, instead of counting each 2048 px image (ADR 0014; the simpler reading of "one 2048 px atlas").
- `machine.json` placeholder positions and sizes follow the modelled bounds. Only values changed.

## Fixed

- The first match renders were too dark because the Blender scene used the generated studio's environment strength instead of the street map's.
- The first bake used Cycles' diffuse colour pass, which is weighted by (1 - metallic), so every metal baked nearly black: the filler cap came out at 0.25 instead of 0.77. Base colour now bakes through emission.

## Known gaps

- Nothing is approved; that is the owner's call, part by part.
- Weak references: the air filter (a manual drawing only), its cover, and the capacitor (round cream can or black box, unresolved). The fuel tap's position and the hose route are unconfirmed.
- The wear is lighter than on the Lagos photos, which show heavy rust and chipping. It can be pushed further once the owner says how worn the machine should look.
- Faces turned away from the key light render dark, in Blender and in the game.
- The frame rate was measured only in headless Edge with software rendering: 102 fps at 4x CPU throttle with the textured model.
