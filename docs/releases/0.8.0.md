# 0.8.0

A MakeHuman hand, and the hand turns the machine.

## Added

- When you drag to turn the view, the mechanic's hand comes in from the bottom right, slides its fingers under the edge of the base plate and turns the machine with your finger. After a long turn it lets go and takes a new hold, hand over hand. It lets go when you lift your finger or put a second finger down to zoom. A tap never brings it in. It holds the base only, at least 20 mm from every part that comes off, and looks the same whatever part your finger started on (plan 0009, ADRs 0016, 0007).
- Small parts (the spark plug, plug cap, fuel tap and capacitor) are pinched between thumb and index fingertip instead of gripped in a fist (plan 0009).
- `assets/hand/LICENCE.md`: every MakeHuman item the hand uses and its licence, all CC0 (ADR 0016).
- `npm run assets` checks the hand file contract of ADR 0016: other morph targets are allowed, and a `pinch` morph needs a `pinch_point` on the node.
- `npm run verify:phone` catches the hand pinching a small part and holding the base during a turn, and checks that the hand lets go when the finger lifts, that a tap shows no hand, and that a turn started on a blocked part shows the same hand.
- `tools/blender/hand.py -- preview <dir>` renders the hand's three poses without baking, for tuning.
- 235 tests, up from 226.

## Changed

- The hand is remade from a MakeHuman body (MPFB 2.0.18 in Blender, approved by the owner in ADR 0016): an adult Nigerian man in his thirties with a working build. Real anatomy and proportions replace the tube-built hand: knuckles, the pad of the thumb, tendons on the back of the hand. MakeHuman's own skeleton poses it open, gripping and pinching, saved as morph targets. Skin, nails and the rolled work-shirt sleeve are baked from the project's own materials; no MakeHuman image ships. 3,912 triangles and 137.6 KB compressed, within the 4,000 and 400 KB budget (plan 0009).
- `tools/blender/hand.py` is rewritten in place for MakeHuman rather than added as `hand_mh.py` as the plan said, so `npm run assets` needed no change. The old scripted hand is in the git history.
- ADR 0008 is superseded by ADR 0017 (hands-on removal, accepted, not yet built); plans that named ADR 0008 now name ADR 0017.

## Known gaps

- The skin has no knuckle wrinkles or veins: MakeHuman's base body is smooth, and its skin images are not used.
- The sleeve is a clean, even tube; a working shirt would be more creased and stained.
- Parts crowd the edge of the base plate on the right (the recoil starter sits on it), so the hand holds the front, left or back of the plate, never the right side.
- The frame rate was measured only in headless Edge with software rendering: 60 fps at 4x CPU throttle with the hand on screen during a turn (it was 113 fps in 0.7.0, measured while turning without a hand), above the 30 fps budget.
- Removal is still the fixed animation: hands-on removal (ADR 0017), sound (ADR 0018) and visible bolts (ADR 0019) are accepted and come in the next plans.
- From the default camera, the far end of the sleeve can just show in the bottom-right corner while the hand holds the base.
