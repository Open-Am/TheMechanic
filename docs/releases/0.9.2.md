# 0.9.2

An easier drop, and customers wait five times longer. From the owner, 2026-10-11: "the drop is a lot more difficult. We need to make it easier. Also, lets extend the time for the patient meter by X5".

## Changed

- Dropping a carried part is easier. The drop zone is no longer just the tray: it is the whole strip of the screen from 64 px above the pull cord down to the bottom, across its full width. While a part is carried the tray lights up with a dashed outline and "Drop it here", and brightens when the part is over it. A tuning of ADR 0020's "dropping it on the tray removes it"; the rule is unchanged.
- Customers' patience is five times longer: Mama Ngozi 750 s (was 150), Alhaji Musa 550 s (was 110), Tunde 400 s (was 80). Data only (`game/public/customers.json`); the patience rule of ADR 0015 is unchanged. A failed pull still costs 20 s of patience, which is now a smaller share of it.

## Fixed

- For a moment at the start of a carry, the hand could trail a part tucked under the tank (the fuel tap) and dip into the machine. Holding a part, the hand now follows it more tightly and more of its points are checked against the machine.

## Added

- 237 tests, up from 236: the drop zone.
